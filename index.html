Average days on market

Same MLS reports · listing to accepted offer, not to closing

Time-to-offer is the clearest read on how hot the market was: 15 days at the 2021 peak, 28 by 2025 — nearly double, and 2026 is tracking at 27 for Jan–Aug. In 2025 single-family homes took 25 days and townhouses/condos 37. Longer is cooler: sellers no longer collect multiple offers in a weekend, which is what let prices keep drifting up without falling.
